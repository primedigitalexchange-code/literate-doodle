Secure deposits and payouts foundation

This operator console uses Stripe Connect Express and Stripe-hosted onboarding. Bank of America is supported only through Stripe's secure external account onboarding and verification flow. This app never asks for Bank of America usernames, passwords, routing numbers, or account numbers.

Supported bank onboarding model

  • Operators create a connected Stripe account.
  • Stripe hosts the onboarding flow and collects bank details securely.
  • Connected-account readiness is checked before this app creates a transfer.
